Privacy policy

How Posterboard handles your data

Effective 22 November 2026

Posterboard ({{ site_url }}) is a private tool run by StructuralPython for writing, scheduling and publishing posts to LinkedIn. It is not open to the public: only people the operator has given an account can sign in. This policy explains what Posterboard stores, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your Posterboard sign-in. A username and a hashed password, set up by the operator. A session cookie keeps you signed in.
  • Your LinkedIn connection. When you choose Connect LinkedIn, LinkedIn sends Posterboard your member ID, your name, your profile picture address and an access token that lets Posterboard post on your behalf. The token is stored encrypted and expires after about 60 days.
  • Your posts. The text you write, the images, videos and PDFs you attach, the times you schedule, and a record of what was published, when, and LinkedIn's ID for the published post.

Posterboard does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. It does not read your LinkedIn feed, connections, messages or comments.

How we use it

  • To publish the posts you write to your own LinkedIn profile, at the time you choose.
  • To show your drafts, scheduled posts and previously published posts.
  • To show who is connected and when the LinkedIn connection needs renewing.

Posterboard only posts what you have written and scheduled. It never posts anything on its own.

Who it is shared with

  • LinkedIn receives the posts and media you publish, through LinkedIn's official API. Once published, a post is governed by LinkedIn's privacy policy.
  • bunny.net hosts Posterboard and stores its encrypted backups. It processes the data only to run the service.

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Cookies

Posterboard uses only the cookies it needs to work: a sign-in session cookie, a security cookie that protects forms (CSRF), a short-lived cookie that checks the LinkedIn sign-in came from you, and one that carries on-screen messages between pages. None is used for tracking.

How long we keep it

  • The LinkedIn token is kept until it expires, or until you choose Disconnect, which deletes it.
  • Drafts and scheduled posts are kept until you delete them. The record of published posts is kept as a history of what went out.
  • Backups are taken every five minutes, with one copy kept per day.

Your choices

  • Disconnect LinkedIn at any time from the dashboard.
  • Revoke Posterboard's access from LinkedIn itself, under Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services.
  • Delete or edit published posts on LinkedIn directly.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, using the contact below.

Security

Every page except this one requires signing in. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS), the LinkedIn token is encrypted at rest, and access is limited to the operator.
